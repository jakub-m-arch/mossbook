A private well-being journal

A journal that stays with you.

Write down your mood, sleep, fears and hard moments in seconds. Mossbook works offline, encrypts every entry on your device and sends nothing to anyone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Writing

A few taps now, the rest whenever you like.

A quick entry takes two taps at most. Add the details later, or never. You mark your mood with the weather, not with a grade.

Mood
From “very hard” to “very good”, with energy and emotions.
Event
What happened, where, what your body felt and what helped.
Fear
The trigger, symptoms, and intensity before and after what helped.
Sleep
Hours, quality, waking up at night.
Nightmare
What it was, and a rewritten version to rehearse (IRT).
Note
Everything else. With tags and search.

Patterns

Observations, not verdicts.

A mood chart and calendar, plus overviews: how you sleep on better days, which time of day tends to be hardest, what actually brings fear down. Overviews only appear once there is enough to go on.

Mossbook does not diagnose and does not tell you how you feel. It shows what you have written, so it is easier to think about or talk about.

No streaks, points or badges. A break from writing is just a break.

For a hard moment

Help without typing a password.

Your plan for a hard moment, crisis lines and exercises (4-7-8 breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding) are right there on the lock screen. The plan is based on the Stanley–Brown safety plan.

You can add a quick entry from there too. Mossbook saves it, but shows nothing from your journal until you unlock it.

If you see a therapist

A report you put together.

Choose the period, entry types and tags, and Mossbook prepares a clear PDF: your entries day by day and your average mood. You see a preview before anything is saved. You decide what goes in and who gets it.

Your data, your files

Data you can take with you.

When you lock it, at most once a day, Mossbook makes an encrypted backup and keeps the last seven. You can also save a full backup as a single file wherever you like, and export the whole journal as readable JSON. The format is publicly documented.

Privacy

Promises you can check.

Anyone can write “we care about your privacy”. Each of the statements below is backed by code that anyone can read.

  1. 1

    It never goes online.

    There is no networking code in the app. An automatic check on every change rejects libraries that could add it.

  2. 2

    Everything is encrypted.

    Every entry, tag, date and setting. XChaCha20-Poly1305, with the key derived from your password by Argon2id. Nothing on disk can be read.

  3. 3

    Only you hold the key. Not even the author.

    When you create your journal you get a 24-word recovery key. Lose both your password and those words, and nobody can recover your entries. That is what real privacy means, and it is better to know from the start.

  4. 4

    No account, analytics or ads.

    There is no server to sign in to and no crash reports sent in the background. There is no paid version that would change any of this.

  5. 5

    Open code, verifiable releases.

    GPL licence. Every installer comes with a checksum and a provenance attestation that ties it to the public code and the build on GitHub. How to check

What Mossbook cannot protect against

A device with malware or remote access, someone who knows your password, or a glance over your shoulder. The full picture is in the threat model.

Questions

Before you start

What if I forget my password?

You open the journal with your 24-word recovery key and set a new password. Write the words on paper and keep them somewhere safe. In Settings you can check at any time that the words you wrote down are correct. Without the password and without the words, nobody can open the journal.

Does Mossbook replace therapy?

No. It is a tool for writing down and organising your own observations, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or recommend treatment. It works well alongside therapy, and the PDF report makes talking to a professional easier.

Where is my data?

In a single encrypted file on your computer, with the automatic backups in a folder next to it. Nowhere else. Moving to a new computer? Save a backup in Settings and open it on the new one.

Why does my system warn me during installation?

Apple's signature costs 99 dollars a year, and signing for Windows needs a separate process. Mossbook does not have them yet. Instead, the code is open and every release has a checksum and an attestation you can verify yourself. On macOS, Homebrew installs without warnings.

Is there a phone version?

Not yet. Android is next. It will work the same way: offline, without even the permission to go online.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. Mossbook is an open-source project under the GPL. There are no ads, no subscription and no data to sell, because none of it leaves your device.
